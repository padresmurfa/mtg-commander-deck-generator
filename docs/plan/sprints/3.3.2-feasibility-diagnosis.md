# Sprint 3.3.2 — Feasibility diagnosis

**Epic:** E3 Solo evaluation · **Gate:** none (G4 is failed and stays failed) · **Status:** `in_progress`

## Goal

**Find out why 33 of 48 real precons are infeasible, before redesigning anything.**

G4 failed in 3.3.1 and its branch says *redesign §3*. This sprint does not do that. It spends one
cheap step on 1.2.1's rule — *go and look before designing around it* — because the number that
turned up beside ρ is strange enough to change what "redesign §3" would even mean.

## Why this and not the redesign

`feasible 15 of 48`. Two-thirds of **real, published, playable** Commander decks fail a gate that
asks for *three mana and one spell cast by turn 5*. That is a very low bar and these are not
pathological decks, so one of three things is true and they want different work:

- the bar or the gate is mis-set, and this is a threshold defect;
- the **stand-in substitution broke the decks** — 11.9 cards a deck lose their effects — and this is
  an artifact of 3.3.1's own instrument;
- the model genuinely cannot play real decks, and *that* is what "redesign §3" has to mean.

Redesigning the objective while the third is unconfirmed risks fitting a new objective to a model
whose problem is somewhere else entirely.

## What is already known, without measuring anything

**The 0.5 bar was calibrated on synthetic decks and its own comment calls them real.**
`mf/objective.h` says the bar is *"set from a measurement, not guessed — sprint 2.3 measured real
decks at 0.86 and 0.94 under naive and careful play"*, and concludes *"half is far below anything a
functioning deck produces"*. The decks measured were `g3_forgiving` and `g3_demanding`, which are
hand-built fixtures in `src/worker.c`. **Thirty-three published precons now produce below it.** The
design made a falsifiable claim about real decks from a measurement of synthetic ones, and reality
has contradicted it. That much needs no new data and is a design amendment regardless of what else
this sprint finds.

The gate itself: `mana >= 3 && spells >= 1` on entry to turn 5 (`MF_GATE_MIN_MANA`,
`MF_GATE_MIN_SPELLS`), §3's "low bar" and the exact negation of §7.8's *"turn 4, one land, nothing
castable — the game is decided"*.

## The decision rule, pre-registered

**Committed before the instrument that would measure it exists**, on 3.3's precedent — the whole
point being that a rule chosen after seeing a distribution is not a rule. Three hypotheses, each
with a signature that distinguishes it from the others. They are **not** mutually exclusive and all
three verdicts are reported whatever the numbers say.

| | Hypothesis | Supported when |
| --- | ---------- | -------------- |
| **H1** | The bar or the gate is mis-set | **At least half of the infeasible decks have a pass rate in [0.35, 0.50)** — they are near-misses, and the failure is a threshold sitting in the middle of the distribution rather than below it |
| **H2** | The substitution broke the decks | **ρ(pass_rate, substituted) ≤ −0.35** across the 48 — the more of a deck the stand-in replaced, the less it can play. The bar is 3.3.1's own effect size, reused rather than freshly chosen |
| **H3** | The model cannot play real decks | Neither of the above, **and** the median infeasible pass rate is **< 0.20**, **and** the zero-substitution controls pass comfortably |

**The controls are the two G3 fixtures**, `g3_forgiving` and `g3_demanding`. They carry **zero**
substitutions and they are the decks the 0.5 bar was set on, which makes them the right comparison
and not merely a convenient one: if they pass at 0.86/0.94 while real decks sit near zero, the
difference is about real decks and not about the gate arithmetic.

**What this sprint may not do.** It may not move `MF_SOLO_GATE_RATE`, `MF_GATE_MIN_MANA` or
`MF_GATE_MIN_SPELLS`. Diagnosing a threshold and changing it in the same sprint is 3.1's forbidden
move — improving an instrument and a bar at once — and H1 being supported is a *finding*, not a
licence. Any change to the bar is pre-registered in a later sprint against decks chosen in advance.

**And it may not re-run G4.** G4 is failed. It stays failed until something about the model changes,
and a re-run here would be the post-hoc rescue 3.3.1 explicitly refused.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] The decision rule above **committed before the per-deck data exists in the tree**
- [ ] Per-deck feasibility emitted by the tool — pass rate, winning rung, substitution counts and
      land count per corpus deck — so the distribution is a record rather than a claim
- [ ] The two zero-substitution controls measured on the same axis, in the same record
- [ ] **All three hypotheses adjudicated against the pre-registered signatures**, and the answer
      recorded whichever way it falls
- [ ] `make check` green at ≥95% line and branch

## Tasks

- [ ] **T1 — Pre-register.** *Done when:* the rule above is committed and no per-deck measurement
      exists in the tree yet.
- [ ] **T2 — The instrument.** A per-deck record from `mfsim validate`: name, win rate, pass rate at
      the winning rung, winning rung, feasible, fitness, `substituted` / `substituted_lands` /
      `commander_substituted`, and the deck's land count. Plus the two controls in the same shape.
- [ ] **T3 — Adjudicate.** Each hypothesis against its signature, all three reported.
- [ ] **T4 — Retro and amendments**, including the §3/§9 amendment that the 0.5 bar's justification
      is measured on fixtures and stated about real decks.

## Risks carried in

- **A diagnosis that ends in "and so we adjust the threshold" is the answer this sprint is most
  likely to want and least entitled to give.** The bar is not moved here.
- **The instrument being diagnosed is the one doing the diagnosing.** Pass rate comes from the same
  solo model whose competence is in question; the zero-substitution controls are the only thing
  separating "the model is broken" from "the corpus is broken", so they are load-bearing.
- **G4 stays failed.** Nothing in this sprint is permitted to un-fail it.
