# Retro — Sprint 3.3.2 — Feasibility diagnosis

**Epic:** E3 Solo evaluation · **Gates:** none. G4 is failed and stays failed

## Headline

> **The bar was calibrated on decks that are nothing like real decks. H1 supported, H2 and H3
> refuted — and G4 is still failed, because none of this touches ρ.**

**The bar sits on the upper shoulder of a unimodal distribution**, with 27 of 48 decks between 0.30
and 0.50. The two decks the bar was set on sit at 0.85 — clear of every real precon but one.

## The adjudication

| | Signature, fixed in advance | Measured | |
| --- | --------------------------- | -------- | --- |
| **H1** the bar or gate is mis-set | ≥ half of infeasible in [0.35, 0.50) | **20 of 33 — 61%** | **SUPPORTED** |
| **H2** the substitution broke them | ρ(pass_rate, substituted) ≤ −0.35 | **+0.2324** | **REFUTED** |
| **H3** the model cannot play real decks | median infeasible < 0.20, controls pass | median **0.376** | **REFUTED** |

**H2 is refuted with its sign reversed, and that is a finding rather than a null.** ρ is **+0.23**,
and ρ(pass_rate, `substituted_lands`) is **+0.2335** — the whole of it is the lands. This is
3.3.1's own pre-registered distortion, *"a substituted land taps unconditionally for its identity →
**overstates**"*, arriving as a measurement instead of a caveat. The stand-in did not break these
decks; it flattered them slightly, and the true pass rates are a little lower than the ones above.

**It is not the mana base.** ρ(pass_rate, lands) = **−0.04**, and feasible and infeasible decks have
the *same* median land count: 38.

## What this does and does not change

**It does not change G4.** ρ is computed over `fitness`, which every scored deck carries whether or
not it is feasible, so feasibility never entered the gate. Recomputed independently from the
per-deck rows: ρ(fitness, win_rate) = **−0.0357**, exactly 3.3.1's figure. H1 explains the strange
number standing *beside* the verdict; the verdict is untouched.

**It does change what "redesign §3" has to mean.** The live hypothesis going in was that the model
cannot play real decks at all. It can — it plays them at a median 0.40 where a hand-built fixture
plays at 0.85. So the §3 question is back to being about the *objective*, not about basic
playability, and the case for the objective's real problem being the absent opponent is
correspondingly stronger.

### Not delivered, deliberately

- **The bar is not moved.** `MF_SOLO_GATE_RATE`, `MF_GATE_MIN_MANA` and `MF_GATE_MIN_SPELLS` are
  untouched. Diagnosing a threshold and changing it in the same sprint is 3.1's forbidden move, and
  H1 being supported is a finding, not a licence.
- **G4 not re-run.** It is failed; a re-run here is the post-hoc rescue 3.3.1 refused.

## Design amendments

| Document | Change | Why |
| -------- | ------ | --- |
| design §9 / `mf/objective.h` | **The 0.5 bar's justification is measured on synthetic fixtures and stated about real decks.** Real precons produce a median 0.40; the fixtures produce 0.85 | The design made a falsifiable claim and reality contradicted it |
| design §9 | The measured distribution, and that the bar sits on its upper shoulder rather than below the mass | A threshold's position relative to the data is the fact about it that matters |
| design §13.2 | `feasible 15/48` explained: a mis-set bar, not an unplayable corpus, and **not** a rescue of G4 | So the next reader cannot take H1 as reason to reopen the gate |
| design §13.3 | **The synthetic fixtures are not a calibration set.** `g3_forgiving` is 38 basic Forests and 62 one-mana bodies; it is a *control*, and using it to set a bar for real decks is what went wrong | The fixtures' purpose needs stating so this cannot recur |
| spec `parameters.objective.solo_gate` | The measured real-deck distribution beside the fixture numbers the bar came from | Values of record |
| spec `validation.precon_fixtures` | The per-deck rows now emitted, and the H1/H2/H3 adjudication | Values of record |

**The sharpest of these needs saying plainly.** §9 argues the bar is *"set from a measurement, not
guessed"* and concludes *"half is far below anything a functioning deck produces"*. Both halves are
true of the decks measured and false of the population they were generalised to. A measurement does
not stop being a guess by being taken — it stops being a guess when it is taken *on the thing the
claim is about*.

## Revised risks

**Scarier.**

- **Every threshold in this project set from a fixture is now suspect.** The 0.5 bar is the one that
  got caught because G4 dragged real decks past it. `MF_GATE_MIN_MANA`, `MF_GATE_MIN_SPELLS`, G3's
  5σ and 2× and `MF_OBJ_MIN_SEPARATION` were all fixed against hand-built decks and none has ever
  met a published list.
- **The feasibility gate is not independent information.** ρ(pass_rate, fitness) = **+0.83**. §9
  asks for "two numbers doing two jobs" — promote on feasibility, rank on fitness — and they are
  substantially the same number.

**Stopped mattering.**

- **"The model cannot play real decks."** Refuted. It plays them; the bar was in the wrong place.
- **The stand-in as a suspect.** Refuted, with the sign reversed: it flatters decks slightly through
  unconditional lands, which is the distortion 3.3.1 predicted and wrote down in advance.
